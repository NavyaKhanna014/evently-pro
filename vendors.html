Rules

A fair deal, written down

Planners pay for certainty. These simple rules keep orders coming to you.

Keep these ready

  • Aadhaar or PAN of the owner
  • Bank account (cancelled cheque or passbook photo)
  • GST number, if you have one (not needed to start)
  • 5 to 10 photos of your gear and godown
  • 2 photos or videos of past events
  • A mobile number that is on WhatsApp

Your promise

  1. On time: setup done at least 2 hours before the event starts.
  2. Same gear: send exactly what was booked, clean and working.
  3. Tell us early: if you cannot do a job, tell us 48 hours before, so we can send a backup.
  4. No side deals: clients we bring book through us, also for their next events.
  5. Good crew: a trained operator stays on site till the event ends.

Questions

Vendors often ask

Is joining really free?

Yes. No joining fee and no monthly fee. We keep 5% only on custom jobs that are done. On Ready Packs you get your agreed rate.

Do I have to take every order?

No. We send the order details on WhatsApp. You say yes or no within 1 hour. Saying no has no penalty.

When and how do I get paid?

The client pays us before setup. We send your money to your bank within 48 hours after the event, with a clear statement.

What are Ready Packs?

Fixed-price sets for small parties, from ₹15,000. You agree your rate once (about 75% of the pack price) and get bookings on your empty dates.

Do I need a GST number?

Not to start. With GST you can also get bigger orders from companies that need GST bills.

Who do I deal with on the event day?

Your crew works with the planner's team at the venue. Bookings, prices and payments always go through us.

Can I keep my own clients?

Yes. Your own clients stay yours. We only ask that clients we bring to you book through us.

What if my gear fails at an event?

Call us at once and we send a backup. The rules for late setup or failed gear are written in the agreement before you join.

Ready to fill your empty days?

WhatsApp or call, every day from 10 am to 8 pm.
